Privacy Policy

This document explains how the privately operated OpenClaw Assistant integration accesses, uses, stores, and shares data from its dedicated Google account.

1. Operator and scope

OpenClaw Assistant is a personal, non-commercial automation run by its individual owner, not a publicly available application. OAuth authorization is restricted to Google accounts explicitly controlled and authorized by that owner. The informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not provide an account sign-up or Google login flow.

2. Google data accessed

  • Gmail: Read message metadata and contents from the dedicated mailbox to classify updates, identify urgent notifications and summarize email. Gmail OAuth access is read-only; the integration is not authorized to send, delete, archive, or alter messages through Gmail.
  • Calendar: Read and manage events and reminders in the dedicated account's assistant calendar.
  • Tasks: Read, create, update, and complete assistant-managed tasks.
  • Drive: Create, read, organize, and update working files in the dedicated Google account.
  • Docs and Sheets: Create and edit notes, documents and structured planning records.

3. Why data is used

Google account data is used solely to assist the account owner with personal productivity: inbox monitoring, time-sensitive reminders, task tracking, calendar planning, document organization, and requested summaries. The integration does not use Google data for targeted advertising, sell the data, or provide it to data brokers.

4. Processing and third parties

Selected email text, metadata or extracted context may be transmitted through the operator's configured AI-model routing provider, including OpenRouter and its selected inference model provider, to classify messages and prepare summaries or suggested actions. Notifications may be delivered through Discord according to the owner's configuration. Data is also processed by Google services and by the owner's self-managed server. GitHub Pages serves this public informational website; private Google messages and OAuth tokens are not published to GitHub.

Those external services have their own privacy practices. The operator should minimize sensitive material submitted for inference and should not use the integration as a sole channel for critical alerts.

5. Storage and retention

Google data remains in the dedicated Google account unless the owner chooses otherwise. The self-managed server stores OAuth credentials in locally protected files or a keyring, and may retain message identifiers, classifications, summaries, processing timestamps, tasks and service logs needed for automation and troubleshooting. Model providers or messaging platforms may retain data under their respective policies. The owner can review and delete local copies and Google Workspace records.

6. Sharing and access controls

The integration is for one owner. It is intended to avoid automatically emailing external recipients, inviting outside attendees, or sharing Drive documents without the owner's authorization. Google information is not intentionally disclosed to other users. No security control eliminates all risk; email and external documents are treated as untrusted input.

7. Control, revocation, and deletion

The account owner can stop the automation, remove stored local credentials and generated files, delete Google Tasks or Calendar records, and revoke the OAuth application's access from Google Account third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authorized Google API access but does not automatically erase already stored summaries or third-party service records.

8. Google API Services Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs is limited to the personal assistant features described above, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable. Google user data is not used to train a general-purpose AI model by the operator; content may nevertheless be processed by the configured inference service as described in section 4.

9. Website data and contact

This static site has no custom analytics, cookies, or forms. GitHub may process standard hosting and access logs under its own policies. For non-sensitive questions about this public site, open an issue in the GitHub repository. Do not include passwords, tokens, private email content or other confidential information in public issues.

10. Policy changes

The owner may update this policy if the assistant's integrations or data-handling practices change. The revision date above will be updated when material changes are made.
